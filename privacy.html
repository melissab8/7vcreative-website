Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

7V Creative is an AI ad studio run by Melissa (Mel) Brown in Manchester. For anything about your personal information, email hello@7vcreative.co.uk.

What we collect

  • When you contact us by email, phone or Instagram: your name, contact details, business details and whatever you choose to send us, such as product photos.
  • When you use our contact form: your name, email, business details, what you are interested in and your message.
  • When you ask for the free guide or tick the box for tips: your email address.
  • When you visit this site: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, make your free sample and deliver any work you order (to take steps towards, or perform, a contract with you).
  • To send the guide and occasional tips, only if you have asked for them (your consent). Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also just reply to ask us to stop.
  • To keep basic business records, such as invoices (legal obligation).

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We use a few trusted services to run the business: Google (email), Cloudflare (website hosting and email forwarding), Web3Forms (delivers messages from our contact form to our inbox) and, for our email list, EmailOctopus. Product photos you send may be processed by the AI tools we use to create your ads.

How long we keep it

Enquiries we don't take further: up to 12 months. Client records: as long as needed for the work and for accounting rules (usually 6 years). Email list: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email hello@7vcreative.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
